## Devlog

I'll think a bit harder about the tutorial. It will start in a dim room with nothing but Millie to interact with. Millie will say a bunch of nonsense, but just enough to get the player intrigued, after that some platforms will appear the player is introduced to their basic controls, moving left/right and jumping. 

Shortly after, some blocks will appear around the room. A command to Press X to mash, then the player talks to Millie again. Millie will hint that the blocks must in the correct order. 

Afterwards, the task panel appears. And a command to Press Z to Unmash. Player should try to understand and discover how to solve it on their own.

### Revamp

In hindsight, after putting it in practice, I think basic movement can illustrated with a basic panel. As it is well-known for the player.

I seem to have found a more interesting way to start the game. Different tasks! Seems like a great idea!
