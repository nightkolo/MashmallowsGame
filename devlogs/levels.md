# Mashmallows (GDD?)

In this puzzle platformer, you take place in a kitchen, your goal is to stick together marshmallows in different shapes to satisfy orders

Meet "Millie" Marshmallow Lady (and "Ettiko" Bubblegum Guy?)

## Mechanics/Game Design Document

### First World - "Sticky Marshmallows"

- **Cherry Bomb**: Special block that explodes upon pressing Z, sending the mashed blocks flying
- **Switches and Doors**: A switch object that activates block doors when a block is landing on it
  - **Regular Block**: A block that exists and doesn't do anything besides mashing and platforming help.

### Second World - "Caramel Core"
- **Twisted Marshmallows**: Special block that rises in size upon unmashing, elevating the blocks higher. It is 1x1 when mashed, and 1xn when unmashed. Is it a twisted marshmallow.
- **Flame** and **Golden Marshmallows**: A flame object that toasts marshmallows into golden to meet the specific order with goldens
- Hazard: Caramel: A hazard that gets marshmallows stuck if touched, except golden marshmallows
- Match Doors: These are special doors that require a certain formation to open, they have bubbles on the form they desire.

## Collectibles?

I figured I need way to make puzzles offer an optional challenge. Such as collectibles to assert a perfectionist player's dominence. Perhaps make it integral to the story, or make it a cute motif in a way.

Something like a basic move counter or timer would not be interesting. I want the collectibles/optional challenge to be something unique to the game design. I needed something truly unique to reward the player for going for a harder challenge.

I proposed: **Srattered throughout the levels, there are collectible special blocks, that must be mashed the player upon completing the order in order to collect. These collectibles special blocks would be large in size and present an optional movement challenge as the player moves to solve the level.** I find this brilliant because it plays on the central mechanic of the game, unlike a boring timer with a score.

Perhaps Millie could interrupt to present extra monologue about these collectibles. She could lightly detail about why the collectibles are worth it. Maybe I shouldn't overthink it, they could be "special orders"? "Delectables"?

This is a weird idea, but how about introducing Ettiko in the game? I could name him "Bubblegum Guy" to coincide with Marshmallow Lady. Maybe he could have an integral role to these collectibles, ey? But I'm not a fan of how large the character scope is gonna be, despite really liking this idea.

These delectables could be used to unlock skins? with cool trails, different SFX, and stuff? And Bubblegum Guy would own a store like Bubblegum Guy's store where he could sell the stuff?

Still working on it.

The collectibles could be 4x4 large, that would present a movement challenge to get the marshmallows into a solved state. Maybe... they could be different unique pre-defined shape formations, but that would be a nightmare to code.
